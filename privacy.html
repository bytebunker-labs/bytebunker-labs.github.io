b. Bytebunker Labs

Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026. This policy covers Bytebunker YouTube, a private tool run by the owner of the Bytebunker Labs YouTube channel, and this website.

What the tool is

Bytebunker YouTube is used only by the owner of the Bytebunker Labs channel. It uploads the channel’s own videos and posts replies to comments on those videos. Every reply is reviewed and approved by the channel owner before it is posted. The tool is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else signs in to it.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By watching or commenting on Bytebunker Labs videos, you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and YouTube’s handling of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

What information the tool uses

  • From the channel owner’s Google account: permission to upload videos and to manage comments on the Bytebunker Labs channel. It has no access to email, files or any other Google service.
  • From viewers: public comments on Bytebunker Labs videos, meaning the commenter’s public channel name and the comment text, and public counts such as views, likes and comments.

How it is used

  • A comment is read so that a reply can be drafted for the channel owner, who decides whether to post it.
  • To draft a reply, the comment text may be processed by an AI model, either one running on the owner’s private server or Anthropic’s Claude service. Commenters’ names are not sent to it.
  • Each selected comment and its draft reply are sent to the channel owner’s private Telegram chat, where the owner approves or changes the reply.
  • Public counts are used for a weekly summary that only the channel owner sees.
  • Information is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to build profiles of anyone.

Storage and deletion

  • Comments are stored on a private server operated by the channel owner, and are deleted from it within 30 days.
  • Copies in the owner’s private Telegram chat stay there until the owner deletes them.
  • The tool’s sign-in credentials are stored only on that server and are never shared.

Sharing

Information is not shared with anyone, except that comment text may be processed by an AI model to draft a reply, and comments and drafts are delivered to the owner’s private Telegram chat, as described above.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your comments on YouTube at any time.
  • The channel owner can revoke the tool’s access through the Google security settings page.
  • To ask for stored comment text to be deleted sooner, contact us as below.

This website

This website uses no cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub’s privacy statement applies to visits.

Contact

Message the Bytebunker Labs YouTube channel, or @bytebunker-labs.bsky.social on Bluesky.

Changes
